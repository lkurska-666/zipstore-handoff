Flying animations

Approved on 26 Nov. All numbers are prototype values.

1 · Why
  • The quiz feels like a small game: every answer gets a reward.
  • Key moments stand out: the dream income answer, choosing a product, paying.
  • The competitor (sellproducts.ai) does the same. We copied its mechanics.
  • It is a hypothesis. We have no baseline yet, so we can't say how much it helps.
2 · What it is
  1. Coins, emoji and sparks fly from the right edge, at the height of the tap. Big moments fly from the middle.
  2. A "🔥 xN" chip in the header, next to a shorter progress bar.
  3. Confetti on two screens.
  4. Picks card: a turning ring behind it and pulsing badges.
Motion is hard to show in a still. Open the prototype (menu "Jump to" opens any screen, ?fx=off turns the effects off).

3b · The streak chip
  • Shown: not on the first screen (no header). Hidden until the first answer, then on every screen to the end of the funnel. After the email step it stops changing.
  • Counts +1: each quiz answer, each main button before the email, choosing a product.
  • Does not count: Next on picks, secondary buttons and links, Back, sound, the first screen button, and anything from the email step on.
  • Normal run: x5 after the quiz, x6, x7, then x8 after choosing a product, and x8 to the end. Side steps (dropshipping explainer, own link) can add more.
  • Direct link to a later screen: the chip starts from the number a normal run would have there.
  • With reduced motion or effects off, there is no chip.
4 · How it works
  • One full-screen canvas above everything (z-index 121), ignores taps, pixel ratio max 2. The loop stops when no particles are left.
  • Particles fall with gravity per frame: coins .42, emoji .32 to .36, sparks .12, confetti .34. They fade as they age.
  • Origin: the right edge of the screen column (10 px inside on a phone; 40 px outside the column on desktop if 90 px are free). Only the height follows the tap. The award and the Reveal fly from the middle, confetti from both side edges.
  • Cannon emoji: one coin in three, the rest from 💸 🤑 💎 🚀 ⭐ ✨ 🔥 💰 🏆 🎉.
  • Chip: 32 px high, 12 px side padding, gold 12/20 bold text, 16 px 🔥, gold 12% fill, 1 px gold 30% ring. Pops in in 0.35 s, jumps for 0.45 s on each +1. The progress bar is 88 px wide, right-aligned.
  • Reduced motion: nothing flies.
  • Removed: drifting icons behind the loaders and on the first screen.
5 · Open points
  • All counts, speeds and timings can be tuned.
  • A feature flag to turn the effects off is a recommendation, not a decision.
  • Placeholders on the picks card: "Supplier secured", "(AI-verified)", "N stores reserved today" and "of 197". They need real data and approval before customers see them.
