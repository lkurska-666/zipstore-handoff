Reveal screen: store window and phone

Timings and speeds are prototype values, not product values.

1 · The animation

The video runs 73 s to show the whole scroll. Phone layout, 393 px wide.

  1. Window: fades in and rises 14 px. 0.7 s, starts at 0.1 s.
  2. "Reserved" sash: slides in from the top right. 0.5 s, starts at 1.0 s.
  3. Phone: fades in and rises 28 px. 0.7 s, starts at 1.8 s.
  4. Scroll: at 3.0 s the page in the phone scrolls once, top to bottom, and stops. The strip at the top moves left at the same time (26 s per loop).

How to build the scroll

  • The mobile page is designed at 375 px. Scale it to the phone screen width and move it with CSS transform (translateY). No scrollTop, no timers.
  • Distance = page height × scale − screen height. Duration = distance ÷ 14 px per second, at least 10 s (65 s in the prototype).
  • Measure again when the container changes size.
  • The phone block must be at least 312 px high, or the card below covers the phone.
  • Easing for the three beats: cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1). Animate only transform and opacity.
  • Reduced motion: no animation, the store shows in its final state.
Placeholders: the 3.0 s start and 14 px per second. At that speed the page takes 65 s, longer than anyone will watch.

One universal page for any product. It shows the person their store and helps to sell. It is not a render of the real store.
